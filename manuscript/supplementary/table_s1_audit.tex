\textbf{Interpretation:} The locking quadrant---deep global spectral compression ($C>50\%$, equivalently $\kappa<0.5$)---is structurally empty across all 33 configurations. Prior injection either amplifies the decoder Hessian trace (pure-lineage priors, $\kappa>1$, $C<0$: spectral expansion) or leaves it vanilla-equivalent (mixed-lineage priors, $\kappa\approx1$, $C\approx0$). This cross-tissue invariant establishes that prior injection alone, regardless of tissue context or prior design, is insufficient to produce perturbation-ready latent organization.
