\section*{Supplementary Table S3: Erythroid Target Gene Set ($\mathcal{T}$, $|\mathcal{T}|=31$)}
\label{tab:s3_targets}

All GRQ and CTCP perturbation experiments target these 31 erythroid genes. Genes selected to span the erythroid program: hemoglobin subunits, master transcription factors, cytoskeletal components, and heme biosynthesis enzymes.

\vspace{0.5cm}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
\textbf{Category} & \textbf{Genes} & \textbf{Count} \\
\midrule
Hemoglobin subunits & \textit{HBB, HBA1, HBA2, HBD, HBG1, HBG2, HBE1, HBZ, HBQ1} & 9 \\
Master TFs & \textit{GATA1, KLF1, TAL1, GFI1B, BCL11A, NFE2, LMO2, MYB} & 8 \\
Cytoskeleton & \textit{EPB42, SPTA1, SPTB, ANK1, SLC4A1, GYPA, GYPB, RHAG, RHCE, RHD} & 10 \\
Heme biosynthesis & \textit{ALAS2} & 1 \\
Chaperone & \textit{AHSP} & 1 \\
Receptors & \textit{KIT, EPOR} & 2 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\vspace{0.5cm}
\textit{Note:} \textit{EPO} (erythropoietin) is not part of the protocol target set: it is not expressed in the blood-stage dataset and was excluded from the GRQ/CTCP target list (\texttt{optionA\_grq\_traversal.py}, T31).
